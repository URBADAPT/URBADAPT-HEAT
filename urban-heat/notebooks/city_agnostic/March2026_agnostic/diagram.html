NB5 Air Conditioning Private
05_AC_0126.ipynb
NUTS3 penetration → zone-level coverage via a logistic income-rank curve, calibrated to reproduce the NUTS3 mean (observed sub-municipal income or 🤖 income emulator)
Two policies under an equal-users constraint: income-targeted catch-up vs uniform uplift

NB6 Early Warning Public
06_EWS_0126.ipynb
Warning days triggered by a baseline-deaths threshold (epi_deaths_file, periodically recalibrated; event_mask fallback where heat deaths are too few — Track B)
Efficacy interpretation follows a city-specific three-tier maturity taxonomy:
  marginal — mature epidemiological HHWS with surveillance (e.g. Rome, Lisbon); added protection only
  intermediate — meteorologically triggered HHWS (e.g. Berlin, Vienna); midpoint of marginal & counterfactual efficacy
  counterfactual — no comprehensive HHAP (e.g. Athens, Copenhagen); deployment from a low baseline
Setup CAPEX graded by tier — zero for mature systems
Institutional ramp-up · gross → net via mortality displacement
Costs: per-capita per-warning-day (default) or per-day city-level model
→ Conditional mortality ↓ on warning days only
⇄ Overlap: efficacy scaled down by population-weighted current-AC coverage — AC shrinks the addressable residual mortality pool
→ Avoided deaths · costs over the CBA horizon
NB7 Urban Street Trees Public
07_vegetation_0126_emulator2.ipynb
Baseline greenness from the Green View Index, by LCZ class
Default policy: regional Q3 catch-up — every region below the city-wide upper quartile receives its ΔGVI shortfall, built LCZ classes only
Two-step cooling: ΔGVI→ΔLST from city/month/LCZ regressions (cooling-only, warming clipped) then ΔLST→ΔT2M via a 🤖 quadratic emulator
Maturity ramp · asset lifetime · equity-weighted allocation variant
Costs: city-specific PLI-adjusted CAPEX + O&M per tree
→ Hazard ↓ ΔGVI → ΔLST → 🤖 → ΔT2M
🌱 Co-benefit: cooling → AC electricity ↓ at a configured reduction rate per GVI point
→ Avoided deaths · costs · equity metrics

NB8 Cost–Benefit Analysis
08_CBA_0126.ipynb
Integrates NB5 + NB6 + NB7 on a common discounted horizon; hazard track read from HAZARD_TRACK
Anchor-year mortality interpolated to annual streams; trees scaled by maturity; gross → net displacement; optional life-years saved

Explicit bookkeeping instead of silent netting:
  AC (NET) — standalone, minus standalone waste-heat penalty
  AC (NET + trees) — combined: lower kWh/user, λy-corrected penalty
  Trees (base O&M) — direct health benefits only

Pareto frontier over policy scales + ICERs along it; benefits by SVI quintile; public vs private cost bearer
→ PV cost, NPV, BCR, cost/death, cost/life-year, ICER, efficient portfolios
